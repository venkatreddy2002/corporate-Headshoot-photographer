Our Journey

2012

Foundation

Lumini was founded with a vision to redefine visual storytelling through precision and artistic excellence. Starting as a small studio, the focus was on delivering uncompromising quality and building strong client relationships.

2016

Expansion

With growing demand, Lumini expanded into advanced commercial photography, introducing premium studio environments, high-end equipment, and a larger creative team to handle complex projects.

2020

Recognition

Lumini became one of the most trusted luxury photography studios, working with corporate brands, high-profile clients, and large-scale campaigns, earning recognition for consistency and craftsmanship.

2025

Innovation

Expanded into premium digital experiences, integrating cutting-edge editing workflows, AI-assisted enhancements, and cloud-based delivery systems to provide faster and more refined results.
